About me

Hello! I'm a passionate application support professional focused on incident resolution, system monitoring, and keeping critical systems reliable.

I specialize in application support and incident management, handling everything from individual user issues to company-wide system outages using tools like DataDog, Salesforce, SQL, and Python.

My approach combines proactive monitoring with rapid troubleshooting to ensure critical business applications stay reliable and performant when organizations need them most.

I'm passionate about the impact reliable systems have on business success, whether it's ensuring seamless transactions, protecting data integrity, or maintaining uptime during critical operations, I'm driven by environments where technical precision directly influences outcomes that matter.
