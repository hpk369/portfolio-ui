  5. 4.8salertP1 incident openedSeverity P1: revenue reporting is affected. Paging the data-platform on-call engineer.

What just happened · An upstream table changed shape. The agent compared it with yesterday's and saw two columns had changed. It opened a P1 for the on-call engineer with a suggested fix.
